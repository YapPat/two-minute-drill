Two-Minute Drill

Down by 4. 1:47 left. Ball on your own 25. Your call, coach.

Every day, everyone gets the same late-game football situation. Call one play at a time, read the defense, manage the clock, and try to drive for the win before time runs out. Then share your drive with friends, spoiler-free.

  • A new daily drill, the same for every player, one try a day
  • Play cards: runs, slants, screens, sideline outs, deep shots, spikes, timeouts and field goals
  • Real clock rules: the two-minute warning, out of bounds, 4th downs and 2-point tries
  • Streaks and stats, practice drills and an archive of past days
