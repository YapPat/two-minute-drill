Two-Minute Drill

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

What stays on your device

Your results, streak, stats, saved drives and settings are stored only on your device. Two-Minute Drill has no accounts. We don't collect your name, email, contacts or precise location, and we never sell your information. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Anonymous daily results

To show how everyone else did on a daily drill (for example, the win rate and the most popular first play), the app may send a small anonymous record when you finish one: a random ID created by the app, the drill number and date, whether you won, your first play and how many plays you ran. This ID isn't linked to your name, your Apple or Google account, or your advertising identifier. The results are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and used only to show these totals.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Two-Minute Drill is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads on some menu and results screens, occasional ads between practice drills, and optional videos you choose to watch for an in-game reward. Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android)
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • information about the ads you see and tap, plus basic device and app information

Google uses this to show, measure and cap ads, to personalize ads where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when iOS asks; you can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Players in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it are asked for consent first, and can change their choice in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Leaderboards (Game Center and Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your daily wins, win rate and longest streak are sent to Apple or Google under your gamer nickname so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies cover this. You can sign out anytime in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, the payment is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Notifications and sharing

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved. When you share a result, your phone's share sheet sends the text you choose; we don't receive it.

Children

Two-Minute Drill is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
