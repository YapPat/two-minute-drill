Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email yp7buenapark@gmail.com and include your phone model and the drill number if something isn't working.

How do I play?

Each drill starts late in the 4th quarter with you trailing. Pick a play card each snap. Runs keep the clock moving; incompletions and plays out of bounds stop it. Score enough to win before the clock hits 0:00. A tie counts as a loss. The story card before each drill hints at what the defense likes to do.

What's the daily drill?

Everyone gets the same situation each day, and a new one unlocks at midnight your local time. You get one try. If you leave partway through, your drive is saved and you can finish it later that day.

How do streaks work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you finish the daily drill, win or lose. If you miss exactly one day, you can keep your streak by watching an optional video before you play the next drill.

What are practice drills and the archive?

Practice drills are extra situations that don't affect your stats. You get a few free each day and can earn more with an optional video. The archive lets you replay the last 7 days for free; older days can be unlocked.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed, and drills work offline. Ads and the "how everyone else did" stats need a connection.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Turn on reminders in the app's Settings, then make sure notifications are allowed for Two-Minute Drill in your phone's Settings → Notifications.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app erases it.
